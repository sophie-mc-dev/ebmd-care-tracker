# EBMD Care Tracker

Vibe-coded with Google AI Studio — a local-first PWA for tracking EBMD (Epithelial Basement Membrane Dystrophy) episodes, daily eye care routine, and treatment adherence, with exportable reports for doctor visits.

> ⚠️ **Not a medical device.** This app is a personal tracking tool, not medical advice. Always follow your ophthalmologist's guidance.

## What it does

EBMD Care Tracker helps you log and understand patterns in corneal erosion episodes and daily eye care routines:

- **Episode logging** — quick-add corneal erosion episodes with severity, affected eye, symptoms, triggers, and optional photos
- **Daily treatment tracking** — log drops, gel, and ointment use, tied to routine anchors (waking up, after lunch, before bed) instead of rigid clock times
- **History** — calendar and list views of all logged episodes and treatments, filterable by date range and type
- **Trends & insights** — adherence rate, episode frequency, humidity/screen-time correlation, most frequent symptoms
- **Cost tracker** — log purchases and see monthly/yearly eye care spend by category
- **Doctor reports** — export a clean summary for appointments
- **Local-first & private** — all data stays in the browser (IndexedDB/LocalStorage), no accounts, no cloud sync

## Tech

Built and deployed with [Google AI Studio](https://ai.google.dev/), running as an installable Progressive Web App (PWA).

## Status

Personal project, actively used and refined. Built to solve a real daily problem: managing EBMD without the routine feeling like a burden.
